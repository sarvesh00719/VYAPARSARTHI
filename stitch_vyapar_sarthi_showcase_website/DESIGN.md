## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
The design system embodies the operational reality of India's retail backbone: pragmatic, deeply trustworthy, culturally rooted, and uncompromisingly modern. It bridges high-velocity Kirana counter ergonomics with elite institutional presentation standards suitable for venture judges and retail shop owners alike. 

The emotional tone balances two vital forces:
1. **Dignity & Absolute Trust:** Small business owners run on razor-thin margins and generational relationships. The UI avoids speculative, playful Silicon Valley tropes in favor of deterministic safety, ledger transparency, and rock-solid financial assurance.
2. **Urgent Modernity & Intuitive Speed:** Bright, high-visibility actions communicate voice-first productivity, immediate stock intelligence, and decisive action without clutter.

### Design Movement: Modern Indic Industrial Fintech
The visual language fuses **Tactile Modernism** with **High-Clarity Utility**:
- **Tactile Soft Surfaces:** Clean layered cards set over light porcelain and warm slate canvases, giving each data card weight and physical palpability.
- **Micro-Contrast Accents:** Rich Saffron-Tangerine draws immediate human focus to primary physical actions (like voice capture and transaction approval), while deep Indigo stabilizes analytical views and financial numbers.
- **Draft-to-Commit Transparency:** Distinctive dual-state surfaces explicitly segregate draft AI suggestions (dotted edges, amber badges, soft frosted tints) from immutable ledger facts (solid emerald confirmations, high-contrast crisp figures).

## Colors

### Chromatic Strategy & Meaning
The palette reflects the daily reality of commerce in India—warm, energized, yet financially grounded:
- **Primary (`#E8590C` Saffron Tangerine):** High-urgency focal point for voice actions, conversion CTA triggers, and primary interactive elements. It carries cultural resonance and standout visibility even on glare-heavy phone screens at billing counters.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A` / `#0F172A` Deep Indigo & Midnight):** Grounding colors for corporate structure, structural navigation, key metrics, and serious transactional data. Conveys institutional permanence.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` Emerald Ledger):** Represents profit, healthy inventory flow, verified POS confirmations, and positive Khata balances. Always paired with `#ECFDF5` for subtle badge fills.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` Midnight Slate):** Anchor for razor-sharp typography, border contrasts, and active iconography.

### Semantic Status Tiers
- **Urgent / Stockout Risk:** Deep Crimson (`#DC2626`) with light scarlet base (`#FEF2F2`).
- **Discretionary / Buy If Budget Permits:** Rich Amber (`#F59E0B`).
- **Base / Verified State:** Slate Indigo (`#1E3A8A`).
- **Voice Live Capture:** Active pulsing gradient combining `#E8590C` to `#F97316` over soft orange backgrounds.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Character
- **Headlines & Financial Numerals (Space Grotesk):** Provides a technological edge with geometric clarity. Crucially, its tabular figures and distinctive glyphs bring punch to Indian Rupee (₹) amounts, inventory units, and high-impact pitch deck statistics.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for warm, open counters, rounded clarity, and supreme legibility across both mobile counters and pitch decks. It smoothly supports bilingual code-switched terms (Hinglish/Marathi phonetic spellings alongside English).

### Numerical Hierarchy & Currency Rules
All currency displays require `metric-currency` styling, ensuring the rupee glyph (`₹`) is rendered directly adjacent to the number without excessive spacing. All sub-labels above metric values (e.g., `TODAY'S REVENUE`, `PENDING KHATA`) must be formatted using `label-caps` in uppercase with subtle letter-spacing for rapid visual categorization.

## Layout & Spacing

### Presentation Canvas & Merchant Dashboards
The layout enforces a dual-purpose architecture:
- **Presentation Mode:** 12-column responsive grid constrained to a 1280px maximum content width. Slides/sections are partitioned into distinct interactive pitch viewports with clean narrative hierarchy.
- **Counter PWA Simulation Mode:** 4-column fluid layout optimized for single-handed mobile usage (360px–430px bounds) or counter tablet views, where bottom action zones host high-frequency voice billing triggers.

### Spacing Cadence
A base unit of `4px` (`0.25rem`) governs all paddings and layout gaps:
- Cards feature internal padding scaled to `space-md` (mobile) and `space-lg` (desktop).
- Quick KPI grids enforce consistent `space-sm` to `space-md` gutters to create compact, cohesive dashboards that present vital retail figures above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

### Surface Architecture
The visual depth system relies on **Tonal Stratification and Diffused Ambient Warmth**, avoiding generic muddy dropshadows:

1. **Base Layer (Canvas):** Soft slate porcelain (`#F8FAFC`), framing interactive elements cleanly.
2. **Resting Cards (Surface Tier 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a crisp border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) paired with an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive & Featured Tiles (Surface Tier 2):** Elevated interactive components, such as Voice Billing triggers, utilize a warm saturated glow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -6px rgba(232, 89, 12, 0.28)`.
4. **Modals & Bottom Sheets (Surface Tier 3):** Grounded floating layers for draft bill verifications, featuring `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
5. **Draft State vs. Persisted State:** Unconfirmed AI calculations (voice transcriptions, draft purchase suggestions) feature an offset ambient badge or dashed border outline (`1.5px dashed #CBD5E1`) to visually reinforce that records have not yet been committed to storage.

## Shapes

### Corner Geometry & Intent
The design system adopts **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Standard interactive elements, inputs, and primary metric cards utilize an `8px` (`0.5rem`) base radius to maintain a modern, friendly, yet disciplined counter feel.
- Large presentation containers and device simulation mockups adopt `16px` (`1rem`) to `24px` (`1.5rem`) outer corners.
- Chips, operational status labels, and quick-filter category selectors adopt full pill shapes (`9999px`) to distinguish actionable tags from structural data containers.

## Components

### 1. Primary Action Button (Voice Billing Hero Button)
- **Visuals:** Saffron-to-Tangerine horizontal gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E8590C 0%, #F97316 100%)`), pure white typography, 16px font size, semi-bold.
- **Structure:** Rounded container (12px), height 56px minimum on mobile for rapid thumb tapping. Includes a distinct microphone icon container with a soft translucent backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Sub-label:** Subtle 12px italicized guide copy beneath the title (e.g., *"Speak in Hindi, English, or Marathi"*).

### 2. Metric & Khata KPI Cards
- **Visuals:** Pure white card surface, rounded 12px corners, crisp 1px neutral outline.
- **Header:** Uppercase metadata in `label-caps` (`#64748B`) flanked by a contextual icon (e.g., Ledger Book for Khata, Bell for Low Stock).
- **Value:** Space Grotesk 28px/32px bold font. Green (`#10B981`) for positive cash/revenue metrics, Vermilion Red (`#DC2626`) for outstanding customer Udhaar/Khata.

### 3. Status Badges & Pills
- **Tiers:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px`, font size `11px`, weight `700`, uppercase tracking.
- **BASE / REPORTED:** Slate Indigo badge (`#EEF2FF` background, `#1E3A8A` text).
- **URGENT:** Light Crimson badge (`#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, dot indicator).
- **BUDGET BOUND:** Warm Amber badge (`#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text).

### 4. Smart Buy Interactive Budget Slider & Simulation Cards
- **Structure:** Slider container featuring dynamic rupee threshold indicators. Left indicator represents current cart value; right indicator sets hard ceiling limit.
- **Track Styling:** Indigo midnight track with vivid Saffron active fill. Thumb has a white core, 4px Saffron border, and subtle drop shadow.
- **Dynamic Rebalancing List:** Cards dynamically change badges from `Buy` (emerald) to `Avoid overbuying` (neutral slate) as the merchant adjusts the total budget slider.

### 5. Draft Bill Review Sheet (Human-in-the-Loop Pattern)
- **Visuals:** Top-pinned or bottom-sheet card with soft amber hairline outline (`1.5px dashed #F59E0B`).
- **Transcript Preview:** Live streaming text block showing mixed Hinglish speech recognition text.
- **Confirm Control:** Dual action bar: Full-width Emerald "Confirm & Print Bill" primary button paired with a secondary slate "Edit Items" ghost action.
